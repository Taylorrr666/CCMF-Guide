COMPULSORY MODULE

The Science of Climate Change

An introduction to the physical science of climate change, covering radiative forcing, climate feedbacks, the carbon cycle, climate models and future climate projections.

Course Overview

The module develops an understanding of the climate science underpinning IPCC reports, the observational evidence for anthropogenic warming, how computer models simulate the climate system, and the uncertainties associated with future climate projections.
