Compulsory Module

Impacts and Adaptation to Climate Change

Explore how climate change creates impacts across regions and societies, how climate risks are assessed, and how adaptation can reduce vulnerability and build resilience.

Course Overview

Impacts and Adaptation to Climate Change examines the realised and anticipated consequences of climate change across regions, sectors and communities. The module explores how hazards, exposure and vulnerability combine to produce climate risk, how uncertainty affects risk assessment, and how adaptation strategies can reduce climate impacts. It also considers extreme-event attribution, droughts, wildfires, human health, loss and damage, climate communication and climate justice.

The module develops an understanding of climate impacts, climate risk and the challenges involved in designing effective adaptation.

06

Core Concepts

Climate impacts depend not only on physical hazards, but also on who and what is exposed and how vulnerable those systems are.

Hazard

A potentially damaging climate-related event or physical phenomenon.

Exposure

People, infrastructure and ecosystems located where climate hazards occur.

Vulnerability

The characteristics that make people or systems susceptible to climate impacts.

Risk

The interaction between climate hazards, exposure and vulnerability.

Adaptation

Actions and decisions that reduce vulnerability or exposure to climate impacts.

Resilience

The ability of systems and communities to resist, absorb, recover and adapt.

Climate Risk & Adaptation Framework

A simplified way of understanding the module is to move from climate hazards to impacts and then to adaptation responses.

1

Hazard

Identify the relevant climate event or trend.

2

Exposure

Identify people, assets and ecosystems at risk.

3

Vulnerability

Evaluate susceptibility and adaptive capacity.

4

Impact

Assess realised or potential consequences.

5

Adaptation

Develop responses to reduce future risk.
