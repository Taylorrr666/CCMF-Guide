Compulsory Module

Business Economics, Climate Change and the Environment

Explore how economic theory helps explain markets, strategic behaviour, inequality, consumer decisions and environmental problems.

Course Overview

This module provides an introduction to economics and its relevance to climate change and the environment. It explores economic growth, strategic interaction, consumer behaviour, institutions, inequality, supply and demand, market failures and environmental policy.
