About CCMF Guide

CCMF Guide is an unofficial student-organised resource designed to make course content and learning materials easier to navigate.

The guide currently focuses on selected compulsory modules from the MSc Climate Change, Management & Finance programme.

Course structures, assessments and teaching materials may change between academic years. Students should always refer to official Imperial College London information for current programme requirements.
