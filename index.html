Compulsory Module

Carbon Accounting & Reporting

Explore how organisations measure, report and manage greenhouse gas emissions, from Scope 1, 2 and 3 accounting to science-based targets, net zero and carbon credits.

Course Overview

Carbon Accounting & Reporting introduces the principles and practice of measuring, interpreting and reporting organisational greenhouse gas emissions. The module covers the GHG Protocol, organisational carbon accounting, Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 emissions, the value chain, science-based targets, net zero, carbon credits and wider sustainability reporting. It also develops the ability to critically evaluate corporate carbon strategies and identify areas for improvement.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of the module, students should be able to understand both the technical construction of carbon accounts and the strategic interpretation of corporate climate disclosures.

01

Understand Carbon Reporting

Explain different forms of carbon reporting and the greenhouse gases included within carbon accounts.

02

Build Carbon Accounts

Construct an elementary organisational carbon inventory using the GHG Protocol.

03

Interpret Scope 1, 2 & 3

Critically interpret organisational emissions across direct, energy-related and value-chain sources.

04

Understand Reporting Frameworks

Recognise important sustainability reporting concepts, including materiality and reporting frameworks.

05

Evaluate Carbon Strategy

Critically assess an organisation's carbon strategy and develop recommendations for improvement.

Core Topics

The module moves from the fundamentals of carbon accounting toward corporate target-setting, decarbonisation strategy and wider reporting.

GHG Protocol

Principles and boundaries used to measure organisational greenhouse gas emissions.

Scope 1

Direct emissions from sources owned or controlled by an organisation.

Scope 2

Indirect emissions associated with purchased electricity and energy consumption.

Scope 3

Indirect upstream and downstream emissions across the corporate value chain.

Science Based Targets

Translate climate science into corporate greenhouse gas reduction targets.

Net Zero & Carbon Credits

Understand residual emissions, removals, carbon credits and corporate net-zero strategies.

Carbon Accounting Workflow

A simplified organisational carbon accounting process can be viewed as a sequence from defining the boundary to reporting and reducing emissions.

1

Boundary

Define the organisation and activities included in the inventory.

2

Activity Data

Collect energy, fuel, travel and value-chain data.

3

Emission Factors

Convert activity data into greenhouse gas emissions.

4

Scopes

Classify emissions across Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3.

5

Strategy

Set targets, reduce emissions and communicate performance.
