About CCMF Guide

CCMF Guide is an unofficial student-organised resource for Imperial College London’s MSc Climate Change, Management & Finance.

The website is designed to make course information easier to browse by bringing together module overviews, assessment structures and lecture materials in one place.

Please always refer to official Imperial College London materials for the latest and most accurate course information.
