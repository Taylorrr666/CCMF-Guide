Compulsory Module

Principles of Finance

Build the foundations of corporate finance and investment decision-making, covering valuation, time value of money, bonds and stocks, risk and return, portfolio theory, CAPM and capital structure.

Course Overview

Principles of Finance provides a foundation in financial theory and introduces the main tools used in corporate finance and investment decision-making. The module develops from time value of money and net present value to bond and stock valuation, capital budgeting, portfolio theory, risk and return, the CAPM, cost of capital and capital structure. The course also connects traditional finance concepts with climate-related applications, including discount rates, green bonds and carbon-related risk.

Learning Outcomes

The module develops the ability to apply financial theory to investment decisions, valuation, risk and corporate financing choices.

01

Value Firms & Investments

Apply valuation techniques to compare firms, projects and investment opportunities.

02

Evaluate Capital Structure

Understand methods of raising capital and evaluate how firms choose between debt and equity.

03

Understand Cost of Capital

Relate financial asset prices and expected returns to a firm's cost of capital.

04

Measure Risk

Measure financial risk and understand its effect on the value of financial instruments.

05

Assess Financial Decisions

Evaluate how changes in financial policies can affect firm value.

06

Apply Finance in Practice

Use quantitative finance tools to analyse real-world corporate and investment decisions.

Core Topics

The course progresses from basic valuation principles to more advanced questions involving risk, portfolios and corporate financing.

Time Value of Money

Discounting, compounding, annuities, perpetuities and the value of cash flows over time.

NPV & Investment Decisions

Net present value, investment decision rules, cost-benefit analysis and capital budgeting.

Bonds & Stocks

Bond pricing, yields, interest rates, dividend valuation and discounted cash flow valuation.

Risk & Return

Expected return, volatility, diversification, systematic risk and beta.

Portfolio Theory & CAPM

Portfolio choice, market portfolios, beta and the Capital Asset Pricing Model.

Capital Structure

Cost of debt, WACC, Modigliani-Miller, leverage and corporate financing decisions.

Financial Decision-Making Framework

A useful way to understand the course is to think of finance as a sequence from identifying cash flows to assessing value, risk and financing decisions.

1

Cash Flows

Identify relevant future cash flows.

2

Discount

Apply the appropriate discount rate.

3

Value

Calculate NPV or asset value.

4

Risk

Evaluate risk, return and cost of capital.

5

Decision

Make investment or financing decisions.
