Compulsory Module

The Science of Climate Change

Explore the physical science behind climate change, including radiative forcing, climate feedbacks, the carbon cycle, climate modelling and future climate projections.

Course Overview

This module introduces the fundamental climate science underpinning the IPCC reports. It examines the observational evidence for anthropogenic warming, the physical processes that shape Earth's climate, the use of computer models to simulate the climate system, and the uncertainties associated with future climate projections.
